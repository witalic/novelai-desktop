Design mockup · Phase 2

Works tab — browse · view · edit

A full Works experience: manage saved works (search, sort, delete), open one into a full-page structured view that reuses the gallery block-stack, and toggle into an edit mode that edits both the gallery structure and the prompt composition. The block-stack renderer is shared with the canvas widget. Filtering by tags/content and DB indexing of the gallery come in a later step.
